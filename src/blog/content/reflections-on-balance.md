Finding balance is something I think about a lot. Between work, side projects, hobbies, and actually resting, it's easy to feel pulled in too many directions.

## The Myth of Perfect Balance

I've come to realize that "balance" isn't a static state you achieve once and maintain forever. It's more like riding a bike—constant small adjustments to keep moving forward without falling over.

Some weeks, work demands more attention. Other weeks, I have space to dive deep into a side project or spend extra time with friends. And that's okay.

## What Helps Me

A few things that have made a difference:

- **Saying no more often** - Not every opportunity is the right opportunity
- **Scheduled downtime** - If it's not on the calendar, it doesn't happen
- **Accepting "good enough"** - Perfectionism is the enemy of done

## Still Learning

I don't have this figured out. But I'm getting better at noticing when things feel off and making adjustments before burning out.
